Product Seeding

a marketing strategy where brands send free products to influencers, creators, or customers without a strict contract or absolute guarantee of a post

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

Product Seeding is a marketing strategy where brands send free products to influencers, creators, or customers without a strict contract or absolute guarantee of a post. Although the (person behind the) brand hopes that they get content or reviews by doing so.

In some sense, product seeding can be thought of as a digital evolution of sending out physical PR packages to hundreds of people at once. Although, often automated through software so that the brand can track who actually posted a video and who just took the free stuff.
